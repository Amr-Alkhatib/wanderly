Do's & Don'ts

  • ✓ Greet shopkeepers with the local phrase — a little effort goes a long way.
  • ✓ Carry cash for local markets — cards aren't widely accepted.
  • ✓ Dress modestly when visiting religious sites.
  • ✕ Don't photograph people without asking permission first.
  • ✕ Avoid peak tourist hours (10am–2pm) at major landmarks.
  • ✕ Don't haggle at fixed-price shops — only at open-air markets.

⏱ Best: 7am – 10am · € Free

Off-beat
⏱ Sundays: 8am – 1pm · € Free entry
