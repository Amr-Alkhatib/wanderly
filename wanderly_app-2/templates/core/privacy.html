Privacy

What Wanderly stores about you, where it lives, and how to get it deleted.

Last updated 18 Nov 2026

The short version

  • You can browse, search and compare destinations without an account. Wanderly sets no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies and loads nothing from other websites.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, the name you give us, your travel preferences and the trips you save. We use them only to run your account.
  • The only cookies are the two that Django needs for security and for keeping you signed in, so there is no cookie banner.
  • Write to {% include "core/_contact_email.html" %} and we will send you a copy of your data or delete it.

2. When you visit the site

Wanderly runs on PythonAnywhere (PythonAnywhere LLP, United Kingdom), whose servers are operated by Amazon Web Services in the United States. Like every web server, it records each request in a log file: your IP address, the date and time, the page you asked for (including any search terms in the address), the response status, the referring page and your browser's user-agent string.

These logs are used only to deliver the site, to find errors and to defend against abuse. We do not combine them with account data or use them to build profiles. PythonAnywhere keeps them for a limited period and then deletes them automatically.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

All fonts, scripts and libraries are served from Wanderly's own server. The site does not embed maps, videos, social media buttons, analytics or advertising, so visiting it does not send your data to any other company.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Wanderly only stores what is strictly necessary to provide the service you request. Under § 25(2) No. 2 of the German TDDDG this does not need your consent, which is why there is no cookie banner.

4. Your account

To create an account you give us your email address and a password, and optionally your first and last name. Sign-up, sign-in, email confirmation and password resets are handled by Supabase Auth (Supabase Inc., USA). Your password is passed to Supabase over an encrypted connection and stored there only as a hash; Wanderly's own database never keeps it. Supabase sends the confirmation and password-reset emails and logs sign-in events, including the IP address, for security.

Wanderly's own database keeps your email address, your name, the date you joined, the time of your last sign-in and the ID that links you to Supabase.

Legal basis: providing the account you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Travel preferences and saved trips

On your account page you can set a daily budget, how much safety matters to you, a travel month and your interests. We use these to rank destinations for you and store them in Wanderly's database. Your name and, if you enter one, an avatar URL are kept in a profile record at Supabase. We only store the avatar URL; Wanderly does not load or show the image.

When you save a destination, we store which destination it is, its title, whether you have marked the trip as completed, and when you saved it. These records are kept at Supabase and linked to your account.

Legal basis: providing these features (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. AI itineraries

Day-by-day itineraries are written by Google's Gemini API (Google LLC, USA). Wanderly's server sends Google only the city, the country, the number of days and a budget figure taken from our own destination data. No information about you is included: not your account, not your preferences and not your IP address, because the request comes from our server, not your browser. Finished itineraries are cached on our server for 24 hours and shared by everyone who opens the same city.

7. Who processes data for us

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. The following providers process data on our behalf, under data processing agreements as required by Art. 28 GDPR:

  • PythonAnywhere LLP (United Kingdom): hosting and server logs. Servers: Amazon Web Services, USA.
  • Supabase Inc. (USA): sign-in, profile records and saved trips. Our Supabase project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1).

Where data reaches a country outside the EU/EEA, it is protected by the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 44–46 GDPR).

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, preferences and saved trips: until you ask us to delete your account. We then delete them from Wanderly's database and from Supabase within 30 days.
  • Removed trips: deleted immediately when you remove them from your saved list.
  • Server logs: deleted automatically by the host after a short period.
  • Cookies and session storage: as listed in section 3.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16), most of which you can do yourself on your account page,
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17) or its use restricted (Art. 18),
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21).

To use any of these rights, write to {% include "core/_contact_email.html" %} from the address on your account. You will hear back within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for us: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart.

10. Other things you should know

You do not have to give us any personal data to use Wanderly. You only need an account to save trips and personalise the ranking. Wanderly makes no automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR); the ranking only decides the order in which cities are shown.

If Wanderly starts processing data in a new way, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
